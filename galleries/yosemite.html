Yosemite
National Park

Granite walls. Ancient forests. Cathedral light. A collection created throughout Yosemite Valley, Glacier Point, Tuolumne Meadows and the high country of the Sierra Nevada.

Yosemite is one of the world's most iconic landscape photography destinations. These photographs were created across multiple visits to Yosemite Valley, Glacier Point, Tunnel View and the high country of the Sierra Nevada, capturing moments of light, weather and season that can never be repeated.
