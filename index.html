About

The land demands stillness before it gives anything.

I work in the intervals between — the hour before light, the last minutes before a storm closes in, the cold that arrives after sunset in high places. These are not accidents. They are appointments.

My practice is built around extended time in terrain that most people move through quickly. The desert teaches patience. The mountain teaches humility. Both teach you to wait.
