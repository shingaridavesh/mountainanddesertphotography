The Artist

A refined eye for the unrepeatable moment.

My work is built around extended time in terrain that most people move through quickly. The high Sierra at four in the morning. The desert floor an hour before the light becomes flat. The forest after rain. These are not places you arrive at quickly.

Each limited edition print in this collection is personally produced to exacting standards using the finest archival methods available — Lumachrome acrylic, Chromaluxe metal, and museum-grade fine art paper. Every piece is crafted for collectors who value rarity, craftsmanship, and images that command a room.

I make myself available seven days a week to speak directly with collectors and answer questions about any work in the collection.
